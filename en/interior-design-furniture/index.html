Interior design and custom furniture for projects in the Balkans

Öder Mimarlık is an interior architecture and furniture design studio based in Istanbul. We assess project requests from the Balkans and Europe: homes, stores, showrooms and trade-fair stands. Share your location, scope and photos, and we will agree on the right way of working together.

What we design

  • Interior architectureSpace planning, concept, materials and coordination from survey to completion.
  • Custom furniture & wardrobesBuilt-in wardrobes, dressing rooms and niches designed to the centimetre.
  • Kitchen cabinetsLayout, storage, worktop and appliance planning in one drawing set.
  • Living rooms & TV wallsWall units, lighting and storage in one calm composition.
  • Children's and teen roomsStudy, storage and play areas planned to grow with the child.
  • FlooringTexture, tone and laying details matched to the whole interior.
  • Showroom & retail furnitureDisplay units and store furniture that put the product first.
  • Trade-fair standsDesign, production and installation planning for fair stands.

How a project from abroad works

  1. Send photos, an approximate plan and the project location.
  2. We clarify scope, priorities and timing in a call or online meeting.
  3. You receive a concept with materials and drawings for approval.
  4. Production, delivery and installation are planned per project, depending on location and scope.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work on projects in the Balkans?

We assess project requests from the Balkans and Europe. The location, the scope and the service you need determine the working method; we clarify it in the first conversation.

Can I order design only?

Depending on your needs, design-focused support or a scope covering design and implementation can be assessed. Scope and suitability are clarified in the first conversation.

How do I get a quote?

Send photos, approximate dimensions and the location through the form or WhatsApp. Once the scope is clear, we share the next steps and the quotation process with you.
